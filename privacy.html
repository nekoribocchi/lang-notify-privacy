Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Lang Notify (the “App”) provides language-learning and reminder features. This policy explains how we handle information related to accounts, cloud sync, AI features, advertising, and deletion requests submitted through this website.

To delete your account: verify your email and submit a request on the account deletion page. Your Google Play subscription will not be canceled automatically. Contact us at nekoribocchi@gmail.com.

1. Information we handle and why

We handle your email address and authentication/session information through Supabase Auth for sign-in and account management; words and learning data you choose to sync and purchase entitlement information for Premium features; and, when you request AI translation, the text, target language, and optional instruction you submit to OpenAI. Supabase stores operational AI usage records for limits and misuse prevention, such as pseudonymous account/session/device/IP matching values, target-language label, outcome or error code, model, token counts, estimated cost, and processing time. These records do not include your submitted text or generated translation. Regular Free words, the cumulative 60-word ledger, and the on-device AI limit stay on your device and are not linked to your account. Google Mobile Ads SDK and UMP are integrated for banner ads; Google may collect or share IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for ads, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention. GitHub Pages may process technical visit information to host and secure this website.

2. Analytics and advertising

Firebase Analytics is not integrated into the current App build. Google Mobile Ads SDK is integrated and may process information for advertising, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention. Collection may vary with consent and ad settings.

3. Third-party services

We use Supabase for authentication and cloud storage, OpenAI for AI translation, Google Mobile Ads and UMP for advertising and consent, and GitHub Pages to host this website. When you request AI translation, your text, target language, and optional instruction are sent to OpenAI. Each service processes information under its own privacy terms.

4. Retention

When deletion is confirmed, we delete the account and linked cloud data and entitlements from active systems we control. Account-linked AI usage allowance records are deleted with the account, and some temporary account rate-limit records are removed. Supabase Free does not guarantee managed backups, but its current documentation also describes temporary daily backups for some Free projects. We have not confirmed whether recoverable copies exist for this project or their deletion schedule, so we do not promise a specific backup deletion period. We may retain minimum records required for applicable accounting, tax, refund, or fraud-prevention obligations for the legally applicable period. Security or aggregate information may be retained only as needed and, where appropriate, without directly identifying you.

5. Account deletion

On the account deletion page, verify your email address and confirm the deletion yourself. The page uses the same response whether or not an account is registered. Regular Free data stored only on your device remains after account deletion. A Google Play subscription is not canceled automatically; cancel it separately through Google Play subscriptions.

6. Children’s privacy

Use the App in accordance with applicable age and consent requirements. Where parental consent is required, obtain it before using the App.

7. Security and policy changes

We limit access to information to what is necessary and use reasonable safeguards. We may update this policy when the App, applicable laws, or third-party services change. The current version will be posted on this page.
