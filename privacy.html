Privacy policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Lang Notify (the “App”) provides language-learning and reminder features. This policy explains how we handle information related to accounts, cloud sync, AI features, advertising, and deletion requests submitted through this website.

To delete your account: verify your email and submit a request on the account deletion page. Your Google Play subscription will not be canceled automatically. Contact us at nekoribocchi@gmail.com.

1. Information we handle and why

  • Account information: Email address and authentication or session information, processed by Supabase Auth for sign-in, identity verification, and account management.
  • Premium cloud data: Words and learning data that you choose to sync, plus information needed to verify purchase entitlements. We use this information to sync across devices, provide Premium features, and prevent misuse.
  • AI feature information: The word or text you enter, the languages involved, and any optional nuance instruction are used for AI translation. When you request an AI translation, this input is sent to the OpenAI API. We also process pseudonymous account, device, session, and IP-derived identifiers and usage counts to enforce limits and prevent misuse.
  • Regular Free data: Regular Free words, the cumulative 60-word usage ledger, and the on-device AI usage limit are stored on your device and are not linked to your account.
  • Advertising information: The Free version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's consent management (UMP). For ad delivery and measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention, the SDK may collect or share IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. Available ad choices are shown in the App based on your region and settings.
  • Website access information: GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may process IP addresses and other technical information when you visit, to operate and secure its services.

2. Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics is not currently integrated into the App. If we add it in the future, we will update this policy and our Google Play Data safety information to reflect the data actually collected.

3. Third-party services

We use Supabase for authentication and cloud storage, the OpenAI API for AI translation, and Google Mobile Ads for advertising. When you request an AI translation, the word or text you enter, the target language, and any optional nuance instruction are sent to OpenAI. Each service processes information under its own privacy terms. For information about advertising SDK data, see Google's Mobile Ads Data safety disclosure.

4. Retention

When account deletion is confirmed, we begin deleting the account, Premium cloud data, entitlement information, and account-linked AI usage information. Our current database hosting plan does not include scheduled database backups. If we introduce backup copies in the future, deletion requests will be applied to those copies and they will expire within 30 days. We may retain the minimum information required for applicable accounting, tax, refund, or fraud-prevention obligations for the legally applicable period. Information used for security or aggregate reporting may be retained only as long as needed and, where appropriate, without directly identifying you.

5. Account deletion

On the deletion page, verify your email address and confirm the deletion yourself. The page uses the same response whether or not an account is registered. Regular Free data stored only on your device remains on that device after account deletion. A Google Play subscription is not canceled automatically; cancel it separately through Google Play subscriptions.

6. Children's privacy

Use the App in accordance with applicable age and consent requirements. Where parental consent is required, obtain it before using the App.

7. Security and changes to this policy

We limit access to information to what is necessary and use reasonable safeguards. We may update this policy when the App, applicable laws, or third-party services change. The current version will be posted on this page.
