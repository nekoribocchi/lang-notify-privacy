What is deleted and what remains

01

Deleted

Your account, Premium cloud words, entitlements, and account-linked AI usage information are deleted. Temporary backups may take up to 30 days to expire.

02

Remains on your device

Regular Free words, the cumulative 60-word usage ledger, and the on-device AI usage limit are stored on your device. Account deletion does not remove them. Delete them separately in the app or on your device if you wish.

03

Purchases and subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. To stop future charges, cancel it separately in Google Play subscriptions.
